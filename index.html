how it works

Four steps. All on-chain.

01

Launch

Launch a pump.fun coin from MINT and lock its rule forever: pick a stock, then short it (the hedge) or long it, at 2x, 5x or 10x.

02

Trade

Every trade of the coin pays creator fees in SOL to the creator's wallet, like every pump.fun coin.

03

Fund

The creator puts those fees into the coin's position on Phoenix, Solana's on-chain perps for stocks, with SOL as collateral.

04

Pay

When the bet pays, the creator closes it and pays holders in SOL, split by how much they hold. One click, public on-chain.

the fine print

Questions.

Who runs a coin's position?

Its creator, from MINT, in their own wallet. MINT never holds a key, a coin or a lamport. Every claim, round, close and payout carries a memo, so each coin's page shows exactly what its creator did and when.

What is the hedge?

A coin set to short a stock gains when that stock falls. If you hold NVIDIA, or anything that moves with it, a coin shorting NVDA pays you SOL in the moments your stock hurts. The same works for Tesla, Apple, the S&P 500 and every other stock Phoenix lists.

Where does the position live?

On Phoenix, Solana's on-chain perps exchange, in the market of the coin's stock, with SOL as collateral. Positions are public. Phoenix asks new wallets to activate with a referral before trading.

Can a position be wiped out?

Yes. Leverage cuts both ways: at 10x, a move of about 10% against the position can liquidate it and the collateral is gone. Holders never lose more than what was put in, but they can get nothing.

How are holders paid?

In SOL, straight to their wallets, split by how much of the coin they hold. Pools and curves are left out. Slices under 0.001 SOL are dropped and the rest re-split.

What does it cost?

pump.fun's normal fees to launch and trade, Phoenix's trading fees on the position, and Solana network fees. MINT takes nothing.

Is this affiliated with NVIDIA?

No. MINT is independent and not affiliated with NVIDIA, any other company it tracks, pump.fun or Phoenix. Each stock is a market on Phoenix that tracks the price of one share. Nothing here is financial advice. Memecoins and leverage can both go to zero.
